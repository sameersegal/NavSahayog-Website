## National recognition for Water Conservation

On **Earth Day**, NavSahyog Foundation is honoured to receive the **Award of Excellence** at **CASCA (Climate Action & Sustainability Conference & Awards) 2026** in New Delhi.

Our **Jal Vriddhi** project was recognised for its unique dual approach: combining experiential learning for village children with the co-creation of **280+ farm ponds** with farmers. By percolating over **20 million litres of water annually**, we are taking measurable steps with rural communities towards climate resilience.

A huge thank you to our partners, stakeholders, and our tireless team. This award isn't a finish line — it's fuel for our mission to build a water-secure future.
